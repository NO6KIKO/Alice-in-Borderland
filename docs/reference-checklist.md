# Detail checklist (Alice in Borderland Tokyo)

Condensed from web research (artofvfx, theluxereview, tokyoweekender, Netflix, TEPCO, OSM). Dimensions ±10–20 %.

## Look
- Season 1: clean but dead — no people, **no electricity** (screens/signs off), vehicles stopped mid-lane, doors open, dropped bags/umbrellas, toppled bikes.
- Season 2: overgrown — weeds in cracks/kerb joints/tactile paving, moss in shaded gutters, ivy/kudzu up poles, guardrails, lower façades (6–10 m), hanging off bridges & expressways; saplings 2–4 m in tree pits; vines over billboards.
- Destruction: collapsed façades, rubble cones, rebar, wires hanging from poles, scorch, burnt bus.
- Animals: panther, tiger (zoo escapees); deer/flamingos optional.
- Card blimps 60–80 m, card face ~10×15 m, drift 1–3 m/s.
- Arenas lit (red laser dots, floodlights, rule screen, countdown); city around dark.
- Grade — day: desaturated teal-grey, haze, warm sun / cool shadow, olive greens. Night: no city light, moonlit blue, stars; colour only from games, fires, Beach neon.

## Street furniture
- Utility poles: grey concrete 10–14 m, every 25–40 m one side, crossarms + 3 insulators at 10–12 m, grey transformer 1.5–2 m lower, 3–8 black cable bundles at 5–7 m sagging 0.3–0.6 m, yellow/black base sleeve to 2 m, step bolts from 2.5 m.
- Street lights 6–8 m grey pole + arm + flat head.
- Signals: horizontal 3-lamp (blue-green / yellow / red) 1.2×0.4 m on arm at 4.5–5.5 m; pedestrian 2-lamp box 0.35×0.7 m at 2.5 m.
- Markings: zebra bars 0.45 m / gaps 0.45 m, no side lines; ◇ 1.5×3–5 m 30–50 m before crossing; 止まれ + stop line; yellow centre lines; red bus lanes.
- Yellow tactile tiles 30×30 cm at kerbs/crossings.
- White pipe guardrail 0.8–1 m, 2 m panels; bollards 0.8 m.
- Manholes 60 cm ornate; U-channel gutters with steel gratings 0.3–0.4 m.
- Vending machines 1.83×1.0–1.2×0.75 m in clusters of 2–5 + recycling bin.
- Convenience store: generic stripes (no logos), glass front 10–15 m, sliding door, parking with wheel stops.
- Vertical signboard stacks 0.5–1 × 6–15 m; nobori flags 0.45×1.8 m.
- Shutters (mostly down), noren, red chochin, A-frame signs, potted plants, plastic food cases.
- Balconies: laundry poles, futons, AC units 0.8×0.55×0.3 m; steel external stairs.
- Torii 2–3 m + hokora in small lots; koban 4×4 m two storeys; post box 0.4×0.4×1.3 m; phone booth 0.9×0.9×2.2 m.
- Bike racks 0.5–0.6 m pitch; mamachari 1.8 m; scooter 1.7 m.
- Vehicles: kei truck 3.4×1.48×1.8, kei car 3.4×1.48×1.7, taxi 4.4×1.7×1.75 indigo, bus 10.5×2.5×3.1.

## Interiors
- Konbini 15×10 m, 2.7 m ceiling: magazine rack at window, gondola aisles 1.35–1.5 m high ~1 m apart, fridge wall at back, 6–8 m counter, ATM.
- Ramen 3–4 m wide: ticket machine by door, L counter 8–12 stools at 0.6 m, open kitchen with stock pots.
- Izakaya: counter + tatami tables, menu strips, beer crates.
- Office: desk islands of 4–8, partitions, carpet tiles, 2.6 m grid ceiling, blinds.
- Apartment: genkan step 15 cm, kitchenette, 6-tatami room (mat 0.91×1.82), fusuma/shoji.
- Station: gates 1.0 m tall at 0.9 m pitch, fare machines, platform screen doors 1.3 m.
